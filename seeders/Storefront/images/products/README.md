# Storefront product photos

One photo per seeded product, named after the product key in `StoreSeeder.php` /
`NetworkSeeder.php`. `SeedsStorefrontFixtures::attachProductPhoto()` copies each onto the
public disk and sets it as the product's primary image; a product with no file here keeps
Storefront's placeholder.

All are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license):
free for commercial use, no permission or attribution required. Unsplash+ photos are
excluded — they need a paid license. Credits are kept anyway so any photo can be traced.

Fetched center-cropped at 800×800 (`?w=800&h=800&fit=crop&crop=entropy&q=78&fm=jpg`).
When replacing one, keep it free of visible brands and logos — two first picks (a Starbucks
bottle and a branded sunscreen) were swapped out for exactly that reason.
